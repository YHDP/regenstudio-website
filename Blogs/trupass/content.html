Regen Studio joins the TruPASS consortium as partner and designated project manager — coordinating a four-year, €2.5 million research programme funded by the Dutch Research Council (NWO) to make Digital Product Passports trustworthy at scale.

From Hydrogen Passports to TruPASS

TruPASS began as a research initiative on hydrogen passports. Hydrogen is a batch product that mixes along the value chain and cannot be traced to the molecule level — yet companies bringing it to the EU market must comply with the Renewable Energy Directive III, CBAM, CSRD, and increasingly the ESPR. The question was clear: how do you build a Digital Product Passport ecosystem that supply chain actors can actually trust?

That question turned out to be bigger than hydrogen alone. The same trust challenges — verifying origin, nesting product identities into derivative passports, integrating with customs systems like CSW-CERTEX — apply across sectors. The project expanded to cover three use cases: hydrogen and its derivatives, container logistics, and ESG reporting.

For Regen Studio, TruPASS is a natural continuation of the Trusted Digital Product Passports position paper we led for FIDES, commissioned by the Dutch Blockchain Coalition. That paper, co-authored with TNO, GS1, TU Eindhoven, HU Utrecht, Credenco, Sphereon, and others, defined a shared vision for trust ecosystems in the circular economy. TruPASS puts that vision to the test in real supply chains.

What TruPASS Investigates

The full project title captures the ambition: Transforming Supply Chains: From Trust to Sustainability with Digital Product Passports & Identities. Awarded in December 2025 under NWO's Knowledge and Innovation Covenant call Digital Identities — A Foundation for Trust in the Digital World, the project is led by Dr. Baris Ozkan from the Information Systems Group at TU Eindhoven's Department of Industrial Engineering & Innovation Sciences.

The research spans three real-world domains:

  • Hydrogen + derivatives — The original use case. Hydrogen product identities can be nested into passports for steel, ammonia, HBI, ethanol, and fertilisers. The new ISO/TS 19870 standard, Hydrogen Europe's liaison role in CEN/CENELEC JTC24, and certification schemes like ISCC, RedCert, and CertifHy all shape the landscape. Partners include HyStandards and FuelFWD.
  • Container logistics — Working with SmartPort and the Port of Rotterdam on traceability and trust across intercontinental transport, border control, and customs integration.
  • ESG & sustainability reporting — With VDL Groep, InnoTractor, P3-Group, and TNO, exploring how DPPs can automate bottom-up reporting rather than relying on top-down, backwards-looking processes.

Researchers from TU Eindhoven, the University of Twente, and HU Utrecht collaborate across disciplines — information systems, organisational trust, and digital identity architecture — with TNO and Credenco providing applied research and technology expertise. The project funds PhD students and onboards students from applied universities, with knowledge dissemination across multiple existing networks.

Regen Studio's Role

Within TruPASS, Regen Studio serves a dual function:

As consortium partner, we contribute our experience in DPP design, trust ecosystems, and the intersection of European regulation (ESPR, eIDAS2, CSRD) with practical implementation — including our participation in European DPP standardisation through CEN/CENELEC, on behalf of the Dutch Blockchain Coalition and FIDES.

As designated project manager, we coordinate the research across all partners — keeping track of milestones, facilitating collaboration between academic researchers and industry partners, and ensuring the project delivers on its promise. This role reflects our position at the centre of the Dutch DPP ecosystem, where we connect policy, technology, and practice.

Why This Matters

The European Union is rolling out Digital Product Passports through the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR). But a passport is only as valuable as the trust people place in it. Two fundamental questions drive TruPASS:

  • What data should be available to whom, when, and for what purpose?
  • How do you develop ecosystem agreements that lead to effective solutions?

These are not purely technical questions. They require understanding how organisations build trust across boundaries — where there is no complete overview of the supply chain, where stakeholders have both shared and conflicting interests, and where no single centre of trust justifies centralisation. TruPASS addresses this by designing general-purpose tools and technologies across its three use cases, aiming to establish wide-spread standardisation and define the social challenges in adoption.

Looking Ahead

Over the next four years, TruPASS will generate research, prototypes, and practical recommendations for building trust into product passports. We will share findings, tools, and lessons as the project progresses.

For Regen Studio, TruPASS represents a natural evolution — from articulating a vision for trusted DPPs to actively building the research foundation that makes it real. We are proud to be part of this consortium and to help coordinate the effort.
