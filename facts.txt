## Who we are
Regen Studio is an independent innovation studio working where technology meets
society and nature. We help organisations navigate regenerative innovation across
five fields, with deep specialism in EU product regulation and digital identity.
Independent advisory: we do not sell software, build platforms, or take commissions
from technology vendors.

## The five fields (and what sits inside each)
1. Energy Transition — smart grids, energy communities, energy justice.
2. Circular Economy — Digital Product Passports (DPP), ESPR advisory, footprinting,
   and the full DPP regulatory landscape (ESPR, CPR, Batteries, PPWR, Toys, Detergents).
3. Liveable Cities — living labs, digital participation, urban greening.
4. Resilient Nature — bioacoustics, reforestation, biodiversity monitoring.
5. Digital Society — digital identity, eIDAS 2.0, EDI wallets, self-sovereign identity,
   verifiable credentials, privacy by design, AI governance.
