    What does revocation do?

    When you revoke AI-processing consent, your data is securely deleted from our active systems within 72 hours. Where your data was encrypted with a per-subject key, we crypto-shred the key (rendering all stored copies cryptographically unrecoverable). Where data is held in plaintext (e.g. contact submissions, newsletter list), we hard-delete the records and propagate the deletion to backups within the same window.

    Audit log entries reference your subject identifier (a UUID derived from your email hash — no plaintext) and persist for accountability under GDPR Art 5(2) / LGPD Art 6(X).

    For full details on what we collect and how we handle it, see the Privacy Policy.
