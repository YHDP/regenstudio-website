What is resilient nature?

Resilient Nature is Regen Studio's work reversing ecological destruction with technology that restores ecosystems and protects biodiversity, through bioacoustics, regenerative agriculture, and biodiversity monitoring.

What resilient nature means

Resilient Nature is the idea that technology should actively help restore ecosystems, rather than settle for doing less harm to them. It covers the tools and practices that rebuild biodiversity, soil, and water systems from the ground up: measuring what is happening in an ecosystem, and farming and managing land in ways that regenerate it rather than deplete it.

Bioacoustics monitoring

Bioacoustics monitoring uses audio recorders to capture the sounds of an ecosystem, bird calls, insect activity, amphibian choruses, and analyses them to track biodiversity health over time. It is non-invasive, scalable, and can run continuously in remote places. We partnered with the Iracambi Biodiversity Centre in Brazil's Atlantic rainforest to design an experiment exploring how sound data can track reforestation and give early warning of ecosystem stress. Try the idea in our AviSonar demo.

Regenerative agriculture

Land management is where a lot of ecological resilience is won or lost. Regenerative agriculture rebuilds soil health, biodiversity, and the water cycle, and healthier soil holds more of every rainfall, which is why it sits at the heart of drought resilience. It connects directly to smart water management.

How Regen Studio helps

We design the monitoring, the experiments, and the innovation processes that let organisations measure and improve ecological health, from acoustic biodiversity tracking to the soil-and-water practices that make landscapes more drought-resilient.
