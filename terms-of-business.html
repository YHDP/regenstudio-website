General Terms of Business

Modular legal framework for all Proposals and Engagements of Regen Studio B.V.

Version 2.0 · adopted 3 June 2026 · English (Dutch prevails on interpretation)

Summary

  • One document, three modules: Universal (A), Commercial (B), Public Sector (C)
  • Module A always leading; Module C prevails over B for public-sector clients
  • Payment term 30 days + statutory commercial interest (Art 6:119a Dutch Civil Code)
  • Liability cap with uncapped carve-outs for intent, GDPR Art 82, IP infringement
  • AI tooling permitted; Client data not used for third-party model training
  • Personal Data? Separate Data Processing Agreement (DPA) prevails
