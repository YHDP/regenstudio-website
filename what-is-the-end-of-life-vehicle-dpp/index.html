What is the End-of-Life Vehicle Digital Product Passport?

The End-of-Life Vehicle Digital Product Passport is the digital record of an end-of-life vehicle's materials, origin, compliance and end-of-life information, required under End-of-Life Vehicles Regulation (2023/0284(COD)). The End-of-Life Vehicles Regulation was formally adopted in 2026, with its Official Journal number still pending. The circularity vehicle passport is created by the Regulation itself and applies two years after the Regulation enters into force.

  • Regulation: ELV Reg. 2023/0284(COD)
  • Status: Regulation adopted 2026, OJ number pending
  • Timeline: Applies 2 yrs after adoption

What the End-of-Life Vehicle DPP is

A Digital Product Passport is a structured digital record, reached through a QR code or data carrier, that carries a product's materials, origin, environmental impact, and end-of-life information across its lifecycle. For end-of-life vehicles, that record is required under End-of-Life Vehicles Regulation (2023/0284(COD)). It gives consumers, recyclers, and regulators verified data, and it supports the circular-economy goals behind the regulation.

What the regulation says

This Regulation replaces the ELV Directive (2000/53/EC) and the 3R type-approval Directive (2005/64/EC) with a single Regulation covering the vehicle lifecycle from design to end-of-life management. Article 13 introduces a 'circularity vehicle passport', a digital tool for the provision of information on the safe removal and replacement of vehicle parts and components. Timeline: proposed 13 July 2023, Council general approach 17 June 2025, Parliament first reading 9 September 2025, provisional political agreement 12 December 2025. Formal adoption by Parliament and Council is still pending. The Regulation will apply 2 years after entry into force. Article 13, COM/2023/451; EC End-of-Life Vehicles overview

Status and timeline

The End-of-Life Vehicles Regulation was formally adopted by the European Parliament and Council in June 2026; its Official Journal number is still pending. The circularity vehicle passport is created by the Regulation itself and applies two years after it enters into force.

Confirmed dates are fixed in a published regulation. Expected and planned dates depend on delegated acts still in progress and are indicative until adoption. For the full picture across every product group, with source citations, see our Digital Product Passport overview.
