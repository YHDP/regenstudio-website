What is smart water management?

Smart water management uses soil-moisture sensors, weather data, and solar-powered pumping to give crops exactly the water they need, when they need it, cutting water use and diesel while building drought resilience. Regen Studio Consultoria LTDA is the Brazil commercial agent for Dutch Flow Technology.

What smart water management is

Smart water management applies water to crops precisely, guided by data rather than habit. Soil-moisture sensors and weather-based scheduling (using the FAO's crop-water-requirement methodology) tell a system how much water a field actually needs, and solar-powered pumps deliver it without diesel. In practice, soil-moisture-sensor-driven irrigation has been shown to cut water use by around 20 to 30% compared with conventional scheduling.

It is the natural complement to regenerative agriculture: regenerative soil stores more of each rainfall, and smart irrigation makes sure any water you add on top is applied only when and where the crop needs it.

Why it matters for drought and emissions

For farms that rely on diesel pumps, switching to solar is a direct emissions cut, and the FAO calls solar irrigation a reliable, cost-effective, and climate-friendly alternative. There is an important caveat the FAO is clear about: cheap solar water removes the cost barrier that used to limit pumping, so without responsible water governance it can accelerate groundwater over-abstraction. That is exactly why it belongs alongside regenerative soil practice, store water in the ground and apply it precisely, rather than simply pumping more.

Together, regenerative practice and smart water management build farms that are more productive and more drought-resilient.
