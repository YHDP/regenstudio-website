What are liveable cities?

Liveable cities are habitats where people and nature thrive together through responsible, human-centred technology. Regen Studio works on them through living labs, digital participation, and urban greening.

What a liveable city is

A liveable city is one where people and nature thrive together, and where technology serves residents rather than extracting from them. In practice that means locally owned energy, clean water and mobility, circular use of materials, climate resilience, and a real say for the people who live there. Regen Studio designs the innovation processes and participation that make that shift happen on the ground.

Living labs

Living labs are real-world environments where innovations are co-created, tested, and refined together with the people who will use them: citizens, businesses, and public institutions. Unlike a traditional pilot, a living lab embeds experimentation into everyday life, trialling smart-energy solutions in actual neighbourhoods or participation tools in real municipal processes. Regen Studio worked on Living Lab Scheveningen, designing the innovation process, managing cases, and mentoring project managers.

Digital participation and urban greening

Liveable cities need residents in the loop and nature back in the street. We design digital participation that gives citizens genuine influence over municipal decisions, and we work on urban greening and climate resilience so cities cool, absorb water, and support biodiversity. The test is always whether the technology strengthens the community and the ecosystem, not just the dashboard.

How Regen Studio helps

We built an eight-phase digital innovation process for the City of The Hague, adopted as departmental policy, and designed the innovation approach for Living Lab Scheveningen. Our method accounts for the parts most innovation ignores: procurement, governance, political cycles, and the handover to the teams who keep it running.
